Civil & Architectural Works

A range of civil and architectural construction works, from structural components to building finishes and installations.

  • Masonry works
  • Roof truss and framing, roofing, flashings, and rain gutters
  • Structural concrete, including steel reinforcements, formworks, structural steel, miscellaneous metal, and anchorages
  • Waterproofing services

  • Concrete finishes, tiling, wall finishes, and ceiling works
  • Construction of drywalls and partitions
  • Hardware installation
  • Aluminum Composite Panel (ACP) installation

Mechanical Works

Mechanical works involving the manufacture and installation of automated and mechanical systems.

  • Provision and installation of fire sprinkler systems

Electrical Works

Electrical installation and related works for lighting, power distribution, and building electrical systems.

  • Electrical wiring and cabling for lights and power supply
  • Service entrance and meter connections
  • Provision and installation of lighting fixtures, switches, receptacles, and outlets

  • Conduit systems and junction boxes

  • Other related electrical works
