01 — ABOUT US

About our company

A&DC CONSTRUCTION CORPORATION is a locally owned and operated construction firm based in Taguig, Manila. We specialize in delivering reliable, safe, and cost-effective construction solutions for residential, commercial, and infrastructure projects across the nation.

Our company is committed to maintaining quality workmanship, meeting project requirements, and providing dependable construction services to our clients.
