01 — ABOUT US

About our company

A&DC CONSTRUCTION CORPORATION is a Filipino-owned and managed general contractor established in 2022 and based in Taguig City. The company provides design, engineering, and construction services for a range of building and development projects.

With a focus on quality service and cost-conscious project delivery, the company brings together its team’s experience, technical capabilities, and industry partnerships to meet client requirements.

02 — OUR STORY

Built on a foundation of quality

The company’s name and logo reflect the founder’s long-held ambition to establish a construction company after years of working as a construction worker. The building and gold elements symbolize A&DC’s goal of helping build clients’ dreams through quality work and pride in every project.
